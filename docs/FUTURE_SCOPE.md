# Future Scope & Production Roadmap

## Project: NEXORA: Intelligent multiagent for business planning startup

---

## 1. Real-Time External Market Data Integration
- **Live Commercial Real Estate APIs**: Direct integration with 99acres, MagicBricks, or Zillow for real-time commercial rent per sq.ft and deposit terms by pincode.
- **Aggregator Platform Scraping**: Live restaurant menu pricing, delivery commission tiers, and review sentiment scraping from Swiggy, Zomato, and Google Maps.
- **Statutory Regulatory Trackers**: Real-time webhook integration with national MCA, GSTN, and FSSAI portals to monitor licensing processing queues and statutory fee revisions.

---

## 2. Advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Layer
- Implement a vector database (e.g. pgvector / ChromaDB) indexing official state industrial policy documents, MSME subsidy schemes (e.g., PMEGP, CGTMSE in India), and municipal zoning master plans.
- Enable agents to cite exact clauses and subsidy schemes relevant to the founder's capital tier.

---

## 3. Collaborative Founder & Investor Workspace
- **Investor Deck Generator**: Automated 12-slide investor pitch deck export (PowerPoint / PDF) directly populated with NEXORA financial models and unit economics.
- **Multi-Stakeholder Collaboration**: Role-based permissions allowing co-founders, CAs, and incubators to review, comment on, and modify plan assumptions in real time.
- **Direct Banking & MSME Loan Integration**: Direct export of business plans formatted to standard Small Business Administration (SBA) or bank loan credit appraisal requirements.
