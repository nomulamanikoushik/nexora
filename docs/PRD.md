# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# NEXORA: Intelligent multiagent for business planning startup
**Tagline**: ?From Capital to Business.?

---

## 1. Problem Statement
First-time entrepreneurs and small-business founders frequently have seed capital and a broad industry sector in mind, but lack structured methodology to:
- Rationally allocate capital across setup, equipment, inventory, staff, marketing, and liquid reserves.
- Identify exact target customer personas and their willingness-to-pay.
- Assess competitor entry barriers and design viable moats.
- Formulate realistic break-even timelines and cash burn curves.
- Navigate statutory licensing lead times (e.g. FSSAI, GST, Shops & Establishment, Fire safety).
- Construct a chronologically sequenced launch roadmap.

Generic chatbots provide disjointed text responses that lack financial consistency and cross-agent validation. NEXORA solves this problem via an autonomous multi-agent pipeline.

---

## 2. Target Audience
1. **First-Time Entrepreneurs**: Aspiring founders seeking structured, actionable launch guidance.
2. **Small Business Owners**: Operators expanding into new micromarkets or launching hybrid physical/cloud models.
3. **Student Founders & Hackathon Innovators**: Users testing startup feasibility before deploying real capital.

---

## 3. Core Capabilities & Value Propositions
- **Dynamic Capital Allocation**: Replaces hardcoded estimates with sector-tailored capital weights across 8 distinct buckets.
- **Explainable Feasibility Scoring**: Transparent composite score (0-100) across 8 explicit dimensions with detailed rationale.
- **Agentic Critic & Revision Loop**: Active CRO agent that rejects dangerous budget assumptions and demands re-planning.
- **Interactive "What If?" Sensitivity Engine**: Real-time parameter tweaking with before-and-after variance diffs.
- **9-Phase Phased Launch Roadmap**: Sequential timeline from idea sensory validation to commercial break-even.
- **Responsible AI Guardrails**: Strict advisory disclaimers labeling all figures as modeled assumptions.
